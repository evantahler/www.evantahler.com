# Patents

![Drawings from Evan Tahler's patents](/images/patents/patents-hero.png)

Before I spent my days building developer tools, I was designing interactive experiences for Blu-ray, Digital Copy, and the web. That work — figuring out how a disc in your living room could know who you are, talk to the internet, and connect you with your friends — produced a stack of patents. Here they are.

## Patents

I'm a named inventor on ten granted United States patents. The images are the front-page drawings from each patent.

<div class="honor-body">

### Event State Monitoring for Multiple Platforms Associated with a User Account

*US Patent 10,271,005 — Granted April 23, 2019*

Keeps track of what a user is doing across many devices tied to one account — a Blu-ray player, a phone, a website — and pushes the right event information to whichever device they're using next.

- [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10271005B2/en)

### System and Method for an Interactive Device for Use with a Media Device

*US Patent 9,522,341 — Granted December 20, 2016*

A companion device (think a toy or second screen) that stays in sync with what's playing on your TV by sharing state through a server, so it can react to the movie as you watch it.

- [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9522341B2/en)

Analytics for physical media: the player keeps a usage log for each disc and reports back which features and content people actually watched.

A server system that lets “smart” disc players save and restore settings and preferences, and share content with other users over the network.

### Method and System for Pairing a Medium to a User Account

*US Patent 8,781,115 — Granted July 15, 2014*

Authentication for physical media: the player reads a disc identifier and its own player identifier and pairs them with your online account, so the disc unlocks your stuff wherever you play it.

- [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8781115B2/en)

### System and Method for Dynamic Video Placement on a Display

*US Patent 8,693,846 — Granted April 8, 2014*

Pre-renders multiple video streams at different screen positions so a player with limited hardware can move picture-in-picture video around the screen in response to user input.

- [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8693846B2/en)

*US Patent 8,521,004 — Granted August 27, 2013*

Manages downloaded content and other data assets stored in a Blu-ray player's local storage across the many discs that want to use it.
